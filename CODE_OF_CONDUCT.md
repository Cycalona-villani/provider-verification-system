# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Everyone participating in this project deserves respectful,
constructive, and harassment-free collaboration.

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions,
code reviews, and communications related to the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat others with courtesy and respect.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas.
- Respect different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Accept feedback and acknowledge mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy.
- Use fictional or appropriately anonymized data in examples.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or intimidation.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting Concerns

## Reporting Concerns

To report a concern, visit the maintainer's GitHub profile
and use a private contact method listed there, if available:

https://github.com/Cycalona-villani

Do not post sensitive information in public issues.
If no private contact method is available, you may open
an issue asking the maintainer to arrange a private
reporting channel. Do not include incident details.

Reports will be reviewed as discreetly as possible.
Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when information
must be shared to investigate or address a concern.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will consider the context and severity of
reported behavior and may:

- Request that the behavior stop.
- Remove or edit inappropriate content.
- Issue a warning.
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions will aim to protect participants
and support respectful collaboration.
